# 0.4.0

The fourth iteration of the stackable 3.5" disk bay. It eases the two fits
the 0.3.0 test print found too tight, the walls' dovetails and the caddy's
spring lock, and relieves the inside corners of the wall joint so the
parts seat without cleaning up.

**Reprint the plates, walls, caddy tray, bezel and joiner plates.** The
rear panel, the 2.5" adapter, the feet and the test coupon are unchanged
from 0.3.0.

## Fixes from the third print

- **The bay goes together by hand.** With both of the wall dovetail's
  slopes at their 0.3.0 angles, the walls were nearly impossible to slide
  in. The steep 15° flare, which stops the walls lifting off, stays. The
  splay, which snaps as the wall slides in, drops from 4° to 1.5°: 0.06 mm
  a tab end instead of 0.17 mm. The flare's faces still slide with no
  clearance; the corners are now relieved (below), so only the faces may
  want a light clean-up.
  ([ADR 0016](https://github.com/kisst/stackable-disk-bay/blob/v0.4.0/docs/adr/0016-third-print-lighter-wall-splay-and-lock.md))
- **Softer spring lock.** The bump on each caddy wall reaches 1.25 mm into
  the top plate, between 0.2.0's soft 1.0 mm and 0.3.0's too firm 1.5 mm.
- **Corner reliefs.** A printed inside corner comes out rounded and the
  sharp corner that seats in it stands off, so the joint does not close
  without clean-up. The wall joint's corners now have a 0.5 mm round
  relief, at the plates' notches and the wall tab roots: a T-bone that
  leaves the fitting face whole. The joiners' dovetails get one too.
- **Glue holes.** The rear panel and bezel joints never gave trouble, so
  they get reliefs at two diagonal corners only, as holes to run a drop
  of superglue into: the walls' rear-panel slots and the bezel's upper
  wall-tab slots.
  ([ADR 0017](https://github.com/kisst/stackable-disk-bay/blob/v0.4.0/docs/adr/0017-corner-reliefs-at-joint-corners.md))

## Known open points

These have not yet been confirmed on a print:

- whether a wall slides in by hand past the 1.5° splay and still stays put
- whether the corner reliefs let the joints close with no clean-up
- whether the 1.25 mm spring bump still clicks clearly
- whether the bezel snaps over its wall tabs and the barb by hand
- whether the centred push tab pushes the caddy out without wedging

See [What the next print has to answer](https://github.com/kisst/stackable-disk-bay/blob/v0.4.0/docs/design.md#what-the-next-print-has-to-answer).
